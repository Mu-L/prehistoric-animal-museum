## Current archive changes

- The untouched downloaded bytes are preserved in `original-glb/`, with their
  SHA-256 recorded in `provenance.json`.
- `normalized-glb/apatosaurus-dinosaur-static.glb` is a technical
  normalization derivative. It has no retained animation, and its presentation
  ground and stone rim have not yet been removed.
- `blender/apatosaurus-restyle-work.blend` keeps the complete imported scene as
  a hidden source reference. Its separate work object excludes the presentation
  ground and stone rim, welds coincident attribute-seam positions, recalculates
  normals, bakes its transform, centres it, and places it on Blender `Z=0`.
- `restyle-v1/apatosaurus-light-restyle-v1.blend` adds a reversible light
  silhouette shape key while retaining both the complete source reference and
  the unchanged prepared body.
- `restyle-v1/apatosaurus-light-restyle-v1-body.glb` is a flattened, body-only
  v1 candidate. Its head/neck transitions, neck base, shoulder, hip, tail root,
  lower-leg taper, and foot length were adjusted. Its original UVs and body
  material are preserved; no rig or animation was added.
- `restyle-v2-material/apatosaurus-material-v2-body.glb` is a material-only,
  body-only derivative of that unchanged v1 geometry. It retains the original
  UV layout and part of the authored map information, while shifting the
  palette to warm olive-brown and yellow-ochre, adding deterministic
  low-contrast mottling, matte roughness variation, and subtle wrinkle/micro
  normal detail. Its three body maps are 1K; no rig or animation was added.
- The published museum derivative further desaturates the green cast into a
  dusty grey-brown olive, adds paired amber-brown eyes, and adds a
  project-authored 15-bone Blender armature with repaired shoulder/hip
  transition weights. A second close-up pass matches the front-leg tops to a
  stationary shoulder bridge. A subsequent skin-detail pass layers irregular
  broad folds, broken secondary wrinkles, fine pebbled grain, and pores over
  the retained normal map, with restrained matching relief in the base colour.
  Its eight-second feeding Idle raises and lowers the head through four neck
  bones while the distal tail responds vertically. The final GLB retains one
  `Idle` clip with eight rotation-only channels.
- Nothing in this candidate directory is approved for runtime use.
