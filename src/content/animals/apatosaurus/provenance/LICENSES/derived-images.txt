Poster, preview and thumbnail derivation record
===============================================

Transparent poster pair generated: 2026-08-05
Responsive previews and thumbnail refreshed: 2026-08-09

The transparent poster pair remains the accepted deterministic first frame of
the replacement Apatosaurus, with transparent pixels outside the model and no
text, controls, logos, or watermarks.

The six shared responsive model previews and their manifest were regenerated
after tightening the live contact shadow around the four-foot cluster. The
shadow uses a 0.34 horizontal scale, 0.9 depth scale, -0.62 horizontal offset,
and 0.46 opacity. The animal geometry remains grounded at y=0; the change only
corrects the shadow footprint and presentation signature.

The collection thumbnail was refreshed in the new habitat. The deterministic
first animation frame was rendered in the 1440 x 900 production viewer with
interface chrome hidden. Pixel comparison against the same frame with the
WebGL canvas hidden located the model, and a 765 x 765 crop at x=549,y=41 was
resized to 320 x 320 and encoded as WebP at quality 82.

The model attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms in model-license.txt continue to
apply to all derived images. The project background retains its separately
recorded terms.
