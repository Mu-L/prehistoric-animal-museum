Poster and thumbnail derivation record
======================================

Poster generated: 2026-07-30
Thumbnail refreshed: 2026-08-09

The transparent poster remains the accepted browser-rendered presentation of
the repaired rigged Tyrannosaurus rex. It captures the live Idle with repaired
foreclaws and the controlled two-pulse bite after the jaw, mouth lining, teeth,
tongue, palate, hip seams, and stationary feet were reviewed.

The collection thumbnail was refreshed after the habitat replacement. The
deterministic first animation frame was rendered in the 1440 x 900 production
viewer with interface chrome hidden. Pixel comparison against the same frame
with the WebGL canvas hidden located the model, and a 773 x 773 crop at
x=537,y=10 was resized to 320 x 320 and encoded as WebP at quality 82.

The model attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms in model-license.txt continue to
apply. The background was generated specifically for this project and is
recorded separately in background-generation.txt.
