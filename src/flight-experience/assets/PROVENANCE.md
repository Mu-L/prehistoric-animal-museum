# Take Flight candidate assets

The new flight experience and its Glide animation are local review candidates.
This document records sources and modifications; it does not grant human approval.

Pteranodon model and the original Idle flight loop are reused unchanged from
`src/content/animals/pteranodon/model/model.glb`. Author: Chistodrako._. /
Oscar López Riviello. Source: [Pteranodon (Animated)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pteranodon-animated-7d7683df41d1405283f160e81a5dff1b).
Model and derived animation license: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The model's original attribution and license remain in the museum's
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and the animal's provenance records.

`pteranodon-glide.json` is a separate animation derivative created by
`scripts/flight/inspect-model.mjs`. It selects a measured wide, nearly horizontal
pose from the repaired Idle loop, fixes translations, and adds a seamless four
second mirrored shoulder/forearm flex (±0.014 radians). The flight runtime blends
this candidate with the unchanged Idle loop. It is artistic motion, not a claim
about reconstructed flight mechanics. `manifest.json` records input/output hashes,
measured bounds, the exact recipe and its pending review status.

The ground retains the museum's existing 1024px land albedo from Poly Haven CC0
scans; its source record remains in SCALE_ENCOUNTER_ASSET_PROVENANCE.md. It is
loaded and owned by the flight session. The earlier unrelated tree atlas and
procedural placeholder crowns are replaced by the same-source samples below.

Terrain, sky and sea are generated by flight code. WorldConfig drives each
terrain/scatter channel. Shallow water samples actual displayed terrain height
into a bounded RGBA8 field; it no longer treats the recipe coast curve as shoreline.
No new remote asset endpoint, audio or external CDN dependency is introduced.

## R3 landscape samples (needs review)

The eight landscape assets are new project-original procedural Blender samples under
CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0: two tree forms with two variants each, three layered rocks, and one
cliff section. They are artistic vegetation/geology, not a paleobotanical reconstruction.
No downloaded scan or third-party model is embedded. The original Pteranodon GLB is unchanged.

`landscape/manifest.json` records Blender version, SHA-256, metres/Y-up dimensions,
LOD triangle counts and conservative cylindrical footprint metadata. The same-source
far tree views are unlit RGBA captures, displayed with dynamic lighting on two vertical
planes and one top plane (six triangles). Their dimensions and anchor match the near mesh.
UV-unwrapped 512px/1k textures contain diffuse colour without direct sunlight, tangent normals
and packed AO/roughness/metallic. The normal bake comes from the authored low-poly
mesh, not an independent sculpted high-poly model. AO baking isolates each source
asset so overlapping authoring samples cannot darken one another.

Reproduce in order with `scripts/flight/assets/create-landscape.py`,
`finish-landscape.py`, `bake-landscape.py`, then `optimize-landscape.mjs`. The optimization
uses the existing glTF Transform/Meshopt/WebP toolchain and preserves named LOD roots.
Run `node scripts/flight/assets/validate-landscape.mjs` for actual exported budgets
and hash checks. Source Blender files and additional review angles remain local; the
candidate GLB and runtime cutouts are imported only by the disabled-by-default flight module.
Human material, silhouette, scale and device-performance approval remain pending.
